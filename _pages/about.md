**Donglin WANG (王东霖)** is a PhD student at the <a href="https://www.waseda.jp/fsci/gips/en/" target="_blank">Graduate School of Information, Production and Systems (IPS)</a>, <a href="https://www.waseda.jp/top/en" target="_blank">Waseda University</a>, supervised by <a href="https://www.waseda.jp/fsci/gips/en/about/faculty/tanaka-eiichiro/" target="_blank">Prof. Eiichiro Tanaka</a>. Prior to his doctoral studies, he received his M.Eng. degree from Waseda University in 2026, and his B.Eng. degree in **Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation (Robotics)** from the <a href="https://coe.cau.edu.cn/" target="_blank">College of Engineering</a>, <a href="https://en.cau.edu.cn/" target="_blank">China Agricultural University</a> in 2024.

My research focuses on **wearable lower-limb assistive robotics** and **human-robot physical interaction**. Specifically, I work on:
* **Mechanism Design & Actuation:** Compact 2-DOF linkage-based ankle and knee exoskeletons, closed-loop torque/admittance control, and compliant human-robot interfaces.
* **Gait Perception & Terrain Adaptation:** Real-time gait phase estimation using IMU sensor fusion and vision-based terrain perception (RGB-D) for stairs and uneven ground.
* **Biologically Inspired Control:** Entrainment-based Hopf Central Pattern Generator (CPG) walking cycle controllers for adaptive assistive torque generation.

Feel free to reach out via email if you are interested in wearable robotics, exoskeleton hardware, or potential research collaborations!
